Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your membership at any time without any hidden fees or penalties.

Do you offer a trial membership?

Yes! We offer a 7-day free trial so you can experience our facilities before committing.

Can I upgrade my plan?

Absolutely! You can upgrade your plan anytime and only pay the difference for the remainder of your billing cycle.

Is personal training included?

Personal training is included in our Premium and Elite plans, or available as an add-on with other plans.
